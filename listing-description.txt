GPT's Puppet Master by X_Bean_X

Open Options with the riverfront icon.

For Windows. Version 1.75.644 supports Cities: Skylines II 1.6.4f1.

GETTING STARTED

• Enable Mod switches all mod effects on or off.
• Six dropdowns control growth, customers, employment, land value, profit and schooling.
• Target sliders work only with Maximize selected and the mod enabled.
• First-install defaults set all dropdowns to Freeze. Checkboxes start on except the two building output controls and Maximum Households, which Freeze Population Growth disables.
• Enable All Options applies defaults and keeps targets. Disable All Options turns the mod off and selects Normal where available. Neither runs a reset.

CITY GROWTH

Choose one mode:

• Freeze Population Growth: stop new residents moving in and pause births.
• Maximize Population Growth: recruit more households and allow normal births.
• Maximize Population Growth and Occupancy of All Commercial & Residential Buildings: also help fill empty homes and commercial premises.
• Maximize Occupancy of All Commercial & Residential Buildings: admit newcomers to fill existing homes and commercial premises, while pausing births.

Recruitment needs buildings, access and space. It does not build homes or fill hotels, service users or extractors.

Freeze Population Growth unchecks and disables both output controls and Maximum Households. Other growth modes unlock them without rechecking them. Enable Mod must be on for effects to work.

DEMAND AND HOUSEHOLDS

• Six Maximum Demand switches cover low-, medium- and high-density residential, commercial, industrial and office buildings. Each holds its building demand at 100%.
• Demand encourages development; it does not supply tenants, workers, customers or profits. Company, storage and extractor demand stay normal.
• Hold Automatic Redevelopment still blocks construction on empty zoned lots. Turn it off when you want development.
• Maximum Households helps fill normal home capacity. It shares City Growth's recruiter, raising occupancy-only batches from 4 to 16. Other growth modes already use 16. Homes and connections are required.
• Freeze Population Growth still blocks new residents and disables Maximum Households. High demand does not override it.
• Turn off Demand+ to use these demand controls. Other demand mods need compatibility checks.
• New options start off for existing users. Disabling them leaves buildings and households in place; demand recalculates on its next update.

CUSTOMERS, LAND VALUE AND PROFIT

Daily Customers, Land Value and Profit offer:

• Normal: let the game update the value.
• Freeze: hold the current value.
• Prevent Decreases: allow increases but stop drops.
• Maximize: use your chosen target.
• Reset to ZERO: keep the value at zero until you change the selection.

Default targets and ranges:

• Daily Customers: 1,000; adjustable from 1 to 100,000.
• Land Value: 100; adjustable from 1 to 10,000.
• Profit: 10,000; adjustable from 1 to 1,000,000.

Targets are not game maxima. Normal or master-off ends overrides without undoing earlier effects.

Reset to ZERO & Freeze Rent holds land value at zero and freezes existing leases. New leases use normal prices. Other land-value modes do not freeze rent.

Customer and Profit overrides change recorded numbers, not shoppers, sales or cash. Past customer records stay unchanged.

MAXIMIZE SHOPPING ACTIVITY

Select under Daily Customers for more shopping chances. It replaces customer-count overrides.

• Shopping still requires needs, money, time, suitable goods and a working route.
• Completed purchases use real goods and payments. Sales are not guaranteed.
• More shopping can increase traffic.
• Profit remains a separate control. Freezing or overriding it can hide changes in reported profit from real trading.

EMPLOYMENT AND OUTSIDE WORKERS

Employment Levels offers Normal, Freeze, Prevent Decreases and Boost Outside Worker Recruitment. Job capacity is separate from actual hiring.

Change Company job counts take priority, including zero. Removing its override starts a fresh Puppet Master baseline.

Recruit extra commuters across all five education levels. They can fill jobs below their qualifications, but not above them.

• Hiring still needs connections, education and suitable jobs.
• Recruitment does not train workers or guarantee skills.
• Commuters are separate from residents, so this can operate while population growth is frozen.
• Extra commuters can increase traffic and simulation work.

SCHOOLING

• Normal Schooling: students enroll, learn and graduate normally.
• Freeze Schooling As Is: pause admissions and progress toward graduation.
• Enrollment Only: allow admissions but pause progress toward graduation.
• Normal Schooling | No Admissions: existing students continue learning and graduating, but no new students join.

Frozen schooling can leave too few qualified workers. Recruitment cannot guarantee workers with the required education.

INDUSTRIAL AND CIVIC OUTPUT

Maximize Occupancy & Production Output of Industrials Buildings helps fill industrial and storage premises at normal job capacity. Offices are excluded. Real workers, materials and storage are needed.

Maximize Occupancy & Services Output of Civic Buildings targets normal staffing, including upgrades and school enrollment rules. It adds no students, patients, detainees or vehicles and changes no user capacity or budgets.

Both output controls:

• Use bounded outside-worker recruitment and take priority over Employment Freeze for their targeted employers.
• Require real workers and the supplies, utilities and access their buildings normally need.
• Cannot guarantee full staffing or output. Shortages and frozen schooling can limit results.
• Work with separate resource, storage and utility controls.

BUILDING PROTECTION AND RESETS

Protect Vacant Buildings guards empty rentable buildings against abandonment caused by poor condition. For eligible buildings already abandoned, it holds the countdown to demolition. Prevent Deterioration protects condition during upkeep.

Reset Vacant Buildings and Reset Abandonment clear eligible poor condition and restart eligible collapse countdowns. Repeat either button whenever needed. Enable the mod and run the simulation to apply resets.

These controls do not reopen abandoned buildings. Fire, disasters and manual bulldozing have separate effects.

TRAVEL, RESOURCES AND STORAGE

Prefer Public Transportation tries transit, walking and cycling first on new citizen routes, then normal choices if needed. Existing trips and service/freight requests stay unchanged. No routes or vehicles are added; failed searches add routing work.

Separate Unlimited switches supply requested Airline Vehicles, Buses, Ferries, Ships, Subways, Trains and Trams. Line targets and schedules stay unchanged. Providers, suitable vehicles, access and servicing still matter. Shared cargo providers and vehicle-making upgrades keep native limits. More vehicles add traffic and simulation work. Switching off restores native limits and may withdraw surplus vehicles. The old combined choice carries over; versions before that switch start these off.

Maximum Resources Available refills business materials, shop goods and service supplies within normal inventory limits, leaving room for industrial output. It prevents extraction from depleting existing oil, ore, fertile land and fish. It adds no money or deposits, regrows no trees and removes no pollution effects.

Maximum Storage refills warehouses and cargo storage with accepted goods up to their existing shared capacity, including applicable upgrades. It does not enlarge storage.

Switching off leaves added goods in place. Extra goods affect production, trade and traffic.

CITYWIDE COVERAGE

Maximum Police Coverage and Maximum Fire Coverage give existing road coverage areas the strongest bonus. They add no provider range, crews or vehicles and do not guarantee responses or prevent crime and fires.

Unlimited Communications & Internet sets the shared telecommunications coverage map to 100% signal quality. It does not increase tower capacity or hardware statistics.

Switching off restores normal coverage updates.

REMOVE THE DEAD & DECAYING

Uses normal game cleanup to remove deceased citizens awaiting pickup.

• Does not send bodies to neighboring cities or create hearses.
• Leaves living citizens, active pickups, bodies in vehicles and bodies already in deathcare alone.
• Does not empty cemetery storage or count deaths again.
• Turning it off stops future cleanup; removed bodies cannot be restored by switching it off.

MAINTENANCE AND UTILITIES

• Protect Road Condition stops further wear on roads and car lanes.
• Protect Park Maintenance keeps parks at their current maintenance level or higher. Repairs can still improve roads and parks.
• Pause City Upkeep Costs stops city-service and map-tile upkeep charges at the next budget update. Other expenses, budgets, maintenance vehicles and deliveries continue.
• Unlimited Electricity, Unlimited Water and Unlimited Sewer are separate switches. They satisfy building demand, clear the matching shortage warnings and remove the related supply efficiency penalty.
• Unlimited Water also supplies clean water and clears dirty-water penalties. Water and sewage can be controlled independently.

Unlimited utilities bypass building supply and network limits. They add no facilities or facility capacity, change no fees and erase no existing pollution. Switching off restores normal supply calculations.

EXISTING SAVES AND COMPATIBILITY

Existing settings are preserved.

Other mods may compete for values. Schooling can limit staffing, supplies can limit output, and Profit overrides can hide trading results. See GitHub for validation and compatibility limits.
